Defect images (AI vision panel)

VisA dataset (Zou et al., ECCV 2022, Amazon) · CC BY 4.0 · cropped. Visual Anomaly (VisA) dataset: Y. Zou, J. Jeong, L. Pemula, D. Zhang, O. Dabeer, “SPot-the-Difference Self-Supervised Pre-training for Anomaly Detection and Segmentation,” ECCV 2022. We cropped four PCB anomaly images and drew each box as the bounding box of the dataset's own ground-truth pixel mask. The boxes are real annotations, not model output.

  • PCB1 · scratch: pcb1/Data/Images/Anomaly/085.JPG, box from pcb1/Data/Masks/Anomaly/085.png (pixel bbox [495, 628, 684, 680])
  • PCB2 · missing: pcb2/Data/Images/Anomaly/040.JPG, box from pcb2/Data/Masks/Anomaly/040.png (pixel bbox [620, 440, 820, 609])
  • PCB3 · bent: pcb3/Data/Images/Anomaly/000.JPG, box from pcb3/Data/Masks/Anomaly/000.png (pixel bbox [332, 418, 619, 562])
  • PCB4 · damage: pcb4/Data/Images/Anomaly/005.JPG, box from pcb4/Data/Masks/Anomaly/005.png (pixel bbox [635, 688, 722, 738])

We checked the NEU surface defect database first. Its homepage gives no license, only a citation request, so we did not use it. MVTec AD was not used because its license is non-commercial.

Data

  • SPC: piston-ring diameters from Montgomery, Introduction to Statistical Quality Control, via the qcc R package.
  • Semiconductor fab data: UCI SECOM (McCann & Johnston, 2008), CC BY 4.0.
  • Defect images: VisA (Zou et al., ECCV 2022), CC BY 4.0; boxes drawn from the dataset's ground-truth masks.
  • Gauge study: NIST/SEMATECH e-Handbook §2.6.1 resistivity probe data (MPC61.DAT), US government work.
  • Torque–angle curves: PyScrew s01 (West & Deuse, TU Dortmund), CC BY 4.0.
  • Vehicle production: OICA 2024.
  • World map: Natural Earth 1:110m land, public domain.
  • Material properties and supplier specs: cited on the Vision and Methods pages.
