Digital Clipboard concept manual: automotive quality workflows

This manual describes concept screens made by chrismai.ai. It is not DataMyte's official documentation. All screens and data are samples.

1. Getting started

  1. Sign in and choose your plant and line from the top bar.
  2. Read the four tiles: first-pass yield, open issues, inspections today and missed inspections.
  3. Click any escalation or corrective action to open its details.

2. Build a checklist

  1. Open Workflow Builder and start from a blank form or the template library.
  2. Drag data elements, such as Barcode, Photos or Signatures, onto the form.
  3. For torque checks, add the wireless torque wrench element and set the nominal value and the lower and upper limits.
  4. Under "When out of spec", require a photo and choose an escalation plan.
  5. Click Preview, then publish the form to the line's tablets.

3. Run an inspection

  1. On the tablet, scan the part barcode.
  2. Tighten each fastener. Readings arrive from the wireless wrench and show PASS or FAIL.
  3. If a reading fails, take the required photo and add a note.
  4. Sign and tap Submit inspection.

4. Escalations

  1. When a reading is out of spec, the supervisor gets an on-device notification and an email.
  2. The supervisor taps Acknowledge and completes the containment task.
  3. If the issue is not closed in time, it escalates automatically to the next level.

5. Corrective action (CAPA)

  1. From an escalation, click Open CAPA. Inspection records and photos are attached.
  2. Work through stages D1 to D8: team, problem, containment, root cause, corrective action, verification, prevention and closure.
  3. Assign tasks with owners and due dates, and update the stage as work is finished.

6. Reports and SPC

  1. Open SPC Analytics to see the control chart, control limits and Cpk for a characteristic.
  2. Red points outside the control limits link to their escalations.
  3. In Reports, search for a serial number to see its full traceability history (Figure 8).
  4. Export to Excel, CSV, Power BI or a PDF audit pack.

7. AI features

  1. CONVERT-ai: upload a PDF, spreadsheet or text file of a paper form. Review the generated workflow and edit it before publishing.
  2. COMPOSE-ai: describe the checklist in plain language. Give feedback in a sentence to create a new version, then confirm it in the final preview.

8. Administration

  1. Set user roles and permissions for each plant.
  2. Choose the languages available to operators.
  3. Review the change history before audits.
