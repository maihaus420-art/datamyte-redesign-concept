From product links to a SaaS suite

DataMyte's site still carries a set of established products: SPC software, data collectors, quality planning, gauge tracking and residual torque tools. This page maps each one to a cloud, multi-tenant concept, with one app screen each.

Concept only. Concept, not DataMyte's actual software. Everything under “today” is summarized from DataMyte's own pages (linked, as of October 17, 2026). The new names, architecture, pricing ideas and screens are ours. Nothing here says DataMyte plans these products.
