What we'd do

  • Rebuild for speed: a lightweight theme on your existing WordPress, so marketing keeps editing, with a performance budget, deferred tags and properly sized images.
  • Make the next step obvious: "Start your 14-day free trial" and "Request a demo" in the hero, the header and the closing section, with your existing forms kept.
  • Accessible by default: target WCAG 2.2 AA: contrast, link names, focus states and a real skip link.
  • An AI chat that works: answers from DataMyte-approved content (pricing, trial, AI features, security) and hands off to sales. The concept shows a scripted version of this.
  • Optional AI-readable layer: structured data plus an llms.txt sketch. Your robots.txt currently blocks AI crawlers such as GPTBot and Google-Extended, so whether to open that up is your call.
  • Tighter copy, same facts: every claim in the concept comes from datamyte.com. No invented stats or testimonials.

Localization and automotive readiness

To show what the shop-floor side could look like, we built an app tour of ten concept screens for a fictional automotive tier-1 plant, including a multi-plant global operations view and a SiC/GaN power-semiconductor quality view: tablet torque inspection, escalation, 8D, SPC and IATF 16949 / PPAP traceability. We also wrote a user manual in the 10 most-spoken languages (Ethnologue 2026) plus German, Japanese and Korean.

  • What it is: concept UI by chrismai.ai, not DataMyte's software. The feature names come from DataMyte's public pages and the data is sample data.
  • What it isn't: the translations are machine-assisted and need native-speaker review before real use. Arabic and Urdu are laid out right-to-left. We make no claim of IATF 16949 certification, AIAG/VDA compliance, or that these workflows have been validated in a plant. That remains DataMyte's and its customers' call.
  • What a real engagement adds: localized marketing pages for priority markets, with hreflang tags and RTL support; native review by vetted subcontracted linguists; automotive landing pages whose wording DataMyte's quality team approves.

The longer-term engineering idea, an agent-ready Digital Clipboard with A2A/MCP, edge sync and AI inspection, is written up as an RFC on the vision page.
