App tour

Fifteen concept screens. The first ten: nine for an automotive tier-1 plant (fictional “Sample Plant A”, seat-frame weld and assembly) and one industry view for wide-bandgap power semiconductors (fictional “Sample Fab S”). Desktop at 1440 px, plus tablet and mobile where the shop floor needs them. Built on one design system: tokens, an 8 pt grid, inline SVG icons, a semantic status scale, and a dark theme with a light theme option. Screens 11 to 15 are the product suite: one new-era SaaS concept for each established DataMyte product.

Concept UI by chrismai.ai, not DataMyte's actual software. Nothing here copies DataMyte screenshots or logos. Feature names come from DataMyte's public pages (cited per screen). Layouts, data and anything marked “Imagined UI” are invented. All data is sample data with no real customer names. “Estimate” marks model-inferred figures and “Real dataset” marks cited public data. See Methods.
